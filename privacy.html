Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What we collect

When you use Reyd, we may collect:

  • Account information: email address and name when you sign up.
  • Content you submit: text and audio files you upload for processing. We process this to deliver the service and do not use it to train models without explicit consent.
  • Usage data: page views, feature usage, and performance metrics via privacy-first analytics (Plausible — no cookies, no personal identifiers).
  • Contact form submissions: name, email, and message via Formspree.

2. How we use your data

We use your data solely to provide, maintain, and improve Reyd. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your submitted content for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data retention

Processed text and audio content is deleted from our servers within 30 days of processing. Version history is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services: Clerk (authentication), Stripe (billing — credit card data is handled entirely by Stripe and never stored on our servers), Formspree (contact form), and Plausible Analytics (privacy-first, no cookies).

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest) to protect your data. We do not store plain-text passwords.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing hello@reyd.app.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@reyd.app.
