Features

Everything you need for Yiddish work

Built for Heimish orthography. Works with the Yiddish you actually write — not just YIVO standard.

Fix & Rephrase

Correct Yiddish grammar and rephrase naturally with one click. Preserves Heimish orthography and spelling conventions.

🔄

Translate

How it works

Three steps. Seconds, not minutes.

From raw text or audio to polished Yiddish — as fast as copy-paste.

1

Paste text or upload audio

Works with any Yiddish text, Word document, or audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A). Record directly in the browser too.

2

Choose your action

Fix grammar, rephrase for clarity, translate to English, or transcribe your audio to text. One click.

3

Copy your result

Clean, polished Yiddish text ready to use. Version history saves every revision so you can always go back.
